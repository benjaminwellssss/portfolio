onebynine.ai (1x9 Systems)

#branding#logo

onebynine.ai (internally called 1x9 Systems) is a technical consulting brand focused on operations modernization and practical, AI-driven workflow automation for small and midsize businesses — deliberately not a flashy "AI toys" pitch. The mark needed to hold that grounded, people-first tone rather than looking like a generic tech-startup logo.

The concept is a 3-by-3 grid — nine squares, reading as "1 of 9" / "one by nine" — with one cell replaced by an X shape, so the icon itself spells out the multiplication "1x9" instead of relying on a wordmark trick alone. It works as a standalone favicon-scale glyph or paired with the "onebynine.ai" wordmark as a full lockup. The palette is a rust orange and dark earthy green, chosen specifically to avoid a generic SaaS look.

The finished system covers full-color and reversed lockups, a standalone icon mark in multiple colorways, and a wordmark built to scale from a browser favicon up to a full site header.
