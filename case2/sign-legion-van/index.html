American Legion Membership Van

#signs#vehicle-graphics#legion

The American Legion, Department of Michigan needed a rolling recruitment and donation vehicle — something that could show up at events, ballgames, and community drives and make the case for membership on sight, not just carry cargo.

Full exterior coverage combines the primary American Legion mark and "Veterans Strengthening America" tagline with sub-program branding — American Legion Baseball, the Legion Riders — plus a full column of military branch seals down the side, laid out to read cleanly at a glance rather than as clutter. Scannable QR codes on the rear windows tie directly to "join" and "donate" actions, turning the van itself into a functional touchpoint instead of a rolling billboard.

Designed and installed personally, start to finish. The van now works as a mobile recruitment tool at Legion events across the state.
