Jesus Loves MSU

#apparel#screen-print#church

Designed while working at Element Church in East Lansing for their presence at Sparticipation 2024, Michigan State University’s annual student-organization fair. The shirt needed a hook strong enough to stop students walking a crowded row of tables — something that read as Spartan gear first and a church shirt second.

The front plays it straight: "Jesus Loves MSU" in Spartan green and white, readable at a glance and impossible not to reread once it lands. The back carries the actual message — "Change the Backyard, Change the World" wrapped around a globe graphic, with the church’s name and site tucked underneath so anyone who liked the shirt actually knew where it came from.

Produced and worn on-site at Sparticipation 2024, doing double duty as event swag and outreach in the same piece of cotton.
