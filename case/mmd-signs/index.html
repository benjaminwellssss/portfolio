MMD Signs: Original Sign Designs

#signs#mmd-signs#banners#ground-signs#exterior-signs#vehicle-graphics

At MMD Signs in Lansing, I designed original artwork for sign jobs of nearly every kind — banners, wall signs, ground signs, truck and trailer wraps, coroplast signs, and more — for local businesses, schools, and community groups. Each job went to the customer as a proof: the artwork shown to scale, with the materials and sizes called out, so it could be approved and then built.

The pieces below are the artwork from those proofs, cropped to the design and its production notes, with client contact details and job numbers removed. They run from January 2023 to May 2025.

The thousands of school yard signs and the vehicle graphics I designed and installed have their own posts: DeWitt Schools Yard Signs, the American Legion Membership Van, and the Michigan State Commander Vehicle.
