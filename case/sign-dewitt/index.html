DeWitt Schools Yard Signs

#signs#yard-signs#schools

While at MMD Signs, DeWitt Public Schools became a recurring client for personalized athlete yard signs — the kind families put in the yard to celebrate a student’s season. Coroplast, 18"x24", built to survive a Michigan season outdoors.

Each sport got its own design: a custom swimmer illustration for swim & dive, a mascot-and-diamond layout for baseball, star-and-paw graphics for cheer, plus versions for football, softball, volleyball, archery, and more, across both the high school and middle school. Every design was templated with a "Student Name" field so the shop could personalize and output them fast, sport by sport, season after season.

This became one of the highest-volume recurring jobs on the floor — roughly ten new designs a year on top of reprints, amounting to thousands of signs printed and sold over the run of the program.
