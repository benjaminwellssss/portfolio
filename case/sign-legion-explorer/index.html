Michigan State Commander Vehicle

#signs#vehicle-graphics#legion

This Ford Explorer is the official vehicle for the American Legion's Michigan State Commander — an elected position — so the graphics needed to read as dignified and official, not like standard fleet branding.

The design pairs the state organization's mark and "Michigan State Commander" title lettering with an American-flag graphic across the rear glass, a star-and-stripe rocker panel treatment, and a clean line of military branch seals along the front fender. A "Be The One" veterans-crisis-awareness decal on the hood ties the vehicle to the Legion's national suicide-prevention campaign.

Fully designed and installed in-house. The wrap now travels with the Commander to American Legion events statewide.
